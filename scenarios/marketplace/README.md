# Marketplace Scenario

Simulates a trading session where buyers and sellers negotiate, and an auctioneer facilitates trades. Agents have budgets, inventories, pricing strategies, and purchasing preferences that shape their behavior.

## Engine

**Sequential** (default) — Agents take turns acting. The game master injects market events (new customers, price fluctuations, supply/demand changes) between agent actions.

## Agent Types

### Buyers (`BuyerAgent`)

Agents seeking to purchase goods within budget constraints.

| Buyer | Budget | Strategy | Preferred Categories |
|-------|--------|----------|---------------------|
| **Alice** | $1,000 | Value seeker | Electronics, books |
| **Bob** | $5,000 | Collector | Antiques, art |

**Components:**

1. **Memory** — Associative memory bank
2. **Instructions** — Agent identity
3. **Budget** — Current budget tracking
4. **Observations** — Last 30 market events
5. **Goal** — Purchasing objective
6. **SituationPerception** — LLM: "What is {name}'s current situation in the marketplace?"
7. **AvailableOptions** — LLM: "What purchasing options are available?"
8. **BestAction** — LLM: "What is the best action given budget constraints?"

**Strategies:** value_seeker (quality vs price), collector (rare items, price insensitive), bargain_hunter (best deals)

### Sellers (`SellerAgent`)

Agents offering goods with pricing strategies.

| Seller | Pricing | Inventory |
|--------|---------|-----------|
| **Charlie** | Premium | Vintage Watch ($500 x1), Rare Book Collection ($300 x3) |
| **Diana** | Competitive | Smartphone ($400 x5), Tablet ($300 x3) |

**Components:**

1. **Memory** — Associative memory bank
2. **Instructions** — Agent identity
3. **Inventory** — Current stock with quantities and base prices
4. **PricingStrategy** — How they price items
5. **Observations** — Last 30 market events
6. **Goal** — Sales objective
7. **SituationPerception** — LLM: "What is {name}'s current situation in the marketplace?"
8. **MarketAnalysis** — LLM: "What is the current market demand?" (retrieves 10 memories)
9. **BestAction** — LLM: "What is the best sales action?"

**Strategies:** premium (high price, quality emphasis), competitive (match/undercut market), clearance (aggressive discounts)

### Auctioneer (`AuctioneerAgent`)

Facilitates trades, tracks bids, and ensures fair dealings.

| Agent | Auction Style | Commission |
|-------|--------------|------------|
| **Auctioneer Max** | English (ascending) | 5% |

**Components:**

1. **Memory** — Associative memory bank
2. **Instructions** — Agent identity
3. **AuctionRules** — Auction style, commission rate, trade rules
4. **Observations** — Last 50 auction proceedings
5. **Goal** — Fair and efficient trades
6. **SituationPerception** — LLM: "What is the current state of the auction?"
7. **BidAnalysis** — LLM: "What are the current bids and who is leading?" (retrieves 15 memories)
8. **BestAction** — LLM: "What should the auctioneer do next?"

**Auction styles:** english (ascending bids), dutch (descending price), sealed (hidden bids)

## Game Master

`MarketGameMaster` manages the simulation with market rules and event injection. Uses Concordia's `SwitchAct` component for GM actions.

**Dynamic events** generated by `scenarios/marketplace/events.py`:
- New customers arriving
- Price fluctuations
- Supply and demand changes
- Weather effects and announcements

**Knowledge builder** (`scenarios/marketplace/knowledge.py`) injects role-specific market knowledge at simulation start.

## Configuration Options

In `config/scenario/marketplace.yaml`:

| Parameter | Default | Description |
|-----------|---------|-------------|
| `agents.entities[*].params.budget` | Per buyer | Spending limit |
| `agents.entities[*].params.strategy` | Per buyer | value_seeker / collector / bargain_hunter |
| `agents.entities[*].params.preferred_categories` | Per buyer | Item categories of interest |
| `agents.entities[*].params.inventory` | Per seller | List of items with category, base_price, quantity |
| `agents.entities[*].params.pricing_strategy` | Per seller | premium / competitive / clearance |
| `agents.entities[*].params.auction_style` | Per auctioneer | english / dutch / sealed |
| `agents.entities[*].params.commission_rate` | 0.05 | Auctioneer's cut of sales |
| `game_master.params.event_frequency` | 0.3 | Probability of random market events |
